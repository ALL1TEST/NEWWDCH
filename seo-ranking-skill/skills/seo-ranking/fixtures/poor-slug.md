# How to Clean a Coffee Maker

A coffee maker that tastes bitter or smells stale usually needs a vinegar cycle, not a replacement. Cleaning a drip machine means descaling the water path and dissolving the coffee oils that coat the basket and carafe: run a brew cycle with a one-to-one mix of white vinegar and water, pause it halfway for about half an hour if the machine allows, then run two or three cycles of plain water until the smell is gone. Once a month is enough for most households, with a quick rinse of the removable parts weekly.

## The Full Routine

1. Fill the tank with equal parts white vinegar and water and run a brew cycle, pausing halfway for 30 minutes if your machine allows it.
2. Empty the carafe and run two to three cycles of plain water, discarding each, until the vinegar smell is gone.
3. Wash the carafe and basket in warm soapy water, scrubbing the brown film inside the carafe with a little bicarbonate of soda on a sponge.
4. Leave the lid open so the tank dries between brews.

## When Cleaning Is Not Enough
