# How to Choose a Mattress for Better Sleep

Knowing how to choose a mattress comes down to three decisions: how firm the surface is, what it is made of, and how both of those match the way you sleep. Get them right, and a mattress becomes a decade of comfortable nights rather than an expensive regret.

## Firmness and Sleep Position

Side sleepers generally need a softer surface that lets the shoulder sink, back sleepers a medium one that holds the spine level, and stomach sleepers a firmer one that stops the hips from bowing the lower back. The difference is not a matter of taste: hybrid mattresses were rated #1 by ConsumerLab's 2025 sleep study, and 92% of owners report better sleep in the first week after switching to one.

## What the Research Says

"The right mattress resolves most back pain within ten days," says Dr. Sarah Whitfield, sleep researcher at the National Rest Institute. "Sleep is a mechanical problem before it is a medical one."

## Materials Compared

Memory foam contours closely to the body but can sleep warm; pocket springs breathe better but can transfer a little more movement across the bed; natural latex lasts a long time but sits at the top of the price range. Hybrid builds try to split the difference, pairing a spring base with foam comfort layers, and suit couples whose preferences differ.

## Trying Before You Buy

Whichever direction you lean, lie down for ten to fifteen minutes in your actual sleeping position before paying — a thirty-second press with a palm tells you almost nothing. Most retailers now offer a trial window measured in months rather than minutes; check the return terms, not just the comfort label.

A mattress is a ten-year relationship. Test it like one.
