# How to Descale a Kettle

Descaling a kettle takes about half an hour and needs nothing more exotic than white vinegar. Fill the kettle roughly halfway with a one-to-one mix of vinegar and water, bring it to a boil, let it stand for twenty minutes to half an hour, then empty it and rinse it well. Boil one or two kettles of plain water afterwards and discard them until the vinegar smell is gone. How often you need to repeat this depends on how hard your water is — roughly every one to three months is enough for most households.

## What You Need

White vinegar, water, and a soft brush for any flakes that survive the boil. Bicarbonate of soda works as a milder alternative for light scale, but vinegar dissolves heavy deposits faster.

## Descaling Step by Step

1. Mix equal parts white vinegar and water and fill the kettle about halfway.
2. Boil the mixture, switch the kettle off, and leave it to stand for 20-30 minutes with the lid closed.
3. Pour the mixture out and brush away any loose flakes.
4. Rinse the kettle, then boil and discard two full kettles of plain water before making drinks in it again.

## Keeping Scale Away

Emptying the kettle after each use and leaving the lid open so the tank dries slows scale build-up noticeably. The same mineral film that furs a kettle also coats other kitchen appliances, so it is worth folding a descaling habit into the same routine as [cleaning an air fryer](/how-to-clean-an-air-fryer) and wiping down the coffee machine.
