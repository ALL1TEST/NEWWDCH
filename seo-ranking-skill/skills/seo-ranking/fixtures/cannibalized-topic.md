# Best Air Fryers 2025: Top Picks

Choosing among the best air fryers in 2025 mostly comes down to four things: how much food fits in the basket, how much power the element draws, how you set the time and temperature, and how easily the parts clean. Families tend to outgrow small baskets within months, while single-person households often pay for capacity they never fill, so the honest starting point is the portion size you actually cook on an ordinary evening.

Controls matter more than a spec sheet suggests. Dial-based models are generally simpler to operate without reading a manual, while touch panels offer presets that remember a favourite combination of time and heat. Wattage mainly affects how quickly the machine recovers heat after the basket is opened mid-cook, which is where underpowered units disappoint.

The guide below groups this year's top picks by household size and cooking style, so you can jump straight to the category that fits your counter and your budget.
