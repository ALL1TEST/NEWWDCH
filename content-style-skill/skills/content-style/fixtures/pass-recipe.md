# Honey-Mustard Roasted Broccoli

A hot oven does most of the work here: broccoli caramelizes at 425°F (220°C) while a two-ingredient glaze turns the crisp edges sweet and sharp. The side is on the table in about 25 minutes, and the only equipment it needs is a sheet pan and a bowl.

## Ingredients

Serves 3 to 4 as a side.

- 1 large head broccoli (about 1 lb / 450 g), cut into bite-size florets
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon whole-grain or Dijon mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- Black pepper to taste

Substitution: maple syrup works in place of the honey, with a slightly softer sweetness.

## Instructions

1. Move a rack to the lower third of the oven.
2. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
3. Toss the broccoli florets with the oil and salt in a large bowl.
4. Spread the florets on the sheet pan in a single layer, cut sides down where they lie flat.
5. Roast for 15 minutes, until the flat faces show dark brown spots.
6. Whisk the honey and mustard together while the broccoli roasts.
7. Drizzle the glaze over the florets when the timer ends.
8. Toss with a spatula until the florets are coated.
9. Return the pan to the oven for 5 more minutes, until the glaze bubbles at the edges.

## Tips

- Crowded florets steam instead of browning; use two pans before you stack one.
- Cut sides down is where the caramelization happens, and it is worth the extra seconds of arranging.

## Storage and Make-Ahead

Leftovers keep about 3 to 4 days refrigerated in a covered container. The glaze softens the crisp edges as they sit, so for the best texture reheat in a 400°F (200°C) oven for a few minutes rather than the microwave. Roasted broccoli softens further when frozen, which makes the freezer a better route to future soup than to a reheated side.
